Questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Every lesson, test, game and tool is free, and you don't need an account.

Where is my progress saved?

In the browser you practise in, on this device. If you clear your browser data or switch computers, your progress starts again.

I have never used a keyboard. Where do I start?

Start with the Keyboard Basics stage in the Typing Tutor. It shows you how to sit, where to rest your hands, which finger presses which key, and how Space, Enter, Backspace and Shift work.

Can I skip lessons I already know?

Yes. Take the placement test, or pass a stage test. Either one clears that stage and opens the next.

What is the difference between gross and net speed?

Gross speed counts every key you press. Net speed counts only the characters you typed correctly, so it is the number that reflects real, usable speed.

How long does it take to learn touch typing?

It varies a lot from person to person. Short daily sessions of 15 to 20 minutes usually beat one long session a week. Expect to feel slower for the first days while your fingers learn the new positions.

Does it work on Windows, Mac and Linux?

Yes. It runs in any modern browser. It is designed for a physical keyboard, so it isn't suited to phones.
